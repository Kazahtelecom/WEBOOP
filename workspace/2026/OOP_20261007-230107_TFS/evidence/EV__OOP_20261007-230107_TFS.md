## Measurements (after the sandbox retry, with the encoding correction)

> **Correction notice (review finding F1).** Row E19 and E21 originally reported a single
> `12.92 MiB` transfer and concluded that the roadmap's `6.4 MB` was "roughly twice too low", on the
> reasoning that the payload was "already compressed" and so could not shrink. **That reasoning was
> wrong and the sign was inverted.** The `python -m http.server` used for that run sends no
> `Content-Encoding` at all: encoded 13 542 472 B exceeded decoded 13 540 917 B by 1 555 B, which is
> HTTP framing, not compression. The same 13 files compress **2.24×** inside the release's own
> `.tar.bz2` (6 757 104 B). A compressed server was therefore written and both delivery modes were
> measured. The corrected conclusion: the documented `6.4 MB` is a **compressed download** figure of
> the same kind as the compressed measurement below, and it is **accurate**. No documentation error
> exists. E19 and E21 are restated; the original numbers are preserved in this notice rather than
> deleted.
